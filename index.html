The Times: App redesign

More of The Times, discovered

A new native app for iOS and Android, designed to help readers discover more of The Times. Each week, 70% of Live app readers visit additional sections such as Life & Style, and 22% explore subsections such as Recipes.

The Times: Article design

Building better article experiences

Redesigned article pages for web, iOS and Android, with new live and comment templates. Clearer layouts and navigation help readers follow developing stories and explore opinion, giving each format its own editorial identity.

The Times: Native audio

Beyond the written word

Bringing Times Radio and podcasts into the app through a native audio player. Designed to make listening easier to discover, with playback that continues as readers browse and explore more of The Times.

About

I work from early ideas through to detailed design, helping teams turn complex problems into experiences that feel straightforward to use. At The Times, that means working closely with product, engineering and editorial colleagues across reading, audio and discovery.
